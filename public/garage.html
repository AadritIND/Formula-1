THE HILL THAT SETS THE TONE.

At Circuit of The Americas, the climb into Turn 1 is more than a postcard angle. Drivers crest the hill under braking, searching for a wide apex that can make or break the first corner. The circuit then turns from fast changes of direction to the tighter stadium complex.

THE PIT WALL IS A SECOND COCKPIT.

Strategy teams turn tyre wear, traffic, weather and rival pace into a call under pressure. A pit window is a moving calculation: stop too soon and the stint runs long; wait too long and the undercut may already be gone.
